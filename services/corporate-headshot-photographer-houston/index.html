Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer on-site corporate headshots in Houston?

Yes. Setups can be arranged at your office or another approved Houston location, planned around your team’s schedule so employees are back to work quickly.

How long does each person take?

Individual time slots are planned during scheduling based on the number of people, the number of expressions or looks, and any selection process. Your quote confirms the schedule.

Can you match headshots for future hires?

Yes. Lighting, background, crop, and editing are documented so new hires can be photographed to match the original look.

How much do corporate headshots cost in Houston?

Pricing depends on headcount, location, session length, and delivery needs. Send an inquiry with team size and location for a quote.

Do you also photograph individual professionals?

Yes. See professional headshots for individuals, or team headshots for larger groups.
