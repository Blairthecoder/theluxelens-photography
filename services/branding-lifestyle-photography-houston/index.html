Frequently Asked Questions

Who are branding sessions for?

They work well for founders, small-business owners, creatives, speakers, service providers, and anyone who needs a cohesive set of current images.

Can we create content for a website and social media?

Yes. The shot plan can include a mix of orientations and negative space so the final library is more useful across web, profiles, press, and social channels.

Do you photograph products too?

Yes. Product and detail images can be included when they support the story of the brand. Share your product list and intended uses during planning.

Will you help plan locations and visual direction?

Yes. Ja'Von can help shape the mood, setting, shot priorities, and mix of portraits and details around your brand and intended uses.

Can a branding session include professional headshots?

Yes. A branding plan can include polished headshots alongside wider portraits, working images, products, and supporting details.

How much does branding photography cost in Houston?

Session options are on the live booking page, and custom brand sessions can be planned through the inquiry form based on your goals and where the images will be used.

What should I prepare for a brand session?

Bring your audience, where the images will appear, reference images, and any products. Those details shape the location, wardrobe, and shot list.

How many images will I receive?

The size of the edited library depends on the plan. Discuss the number of images and formats you need during planning.
