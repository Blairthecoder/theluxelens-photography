How an On-Site Headshot Day Works

1. Define the standard

Your coordinator confirms the visual reference, background, crop, wardrobe guidance, final image count, naming convention, and where the photographs will be used.

2. Build the schedule

We plan employee time slots around team size, location access, setup time, expression variety, and the level of selection or review required.

3. Photograph and deliver

Each person receives direct guidance, while the lighting and framing remain consistent. Final files are edited, organized, and delivered according to the agreed workflow.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many employees can you photograph?

Capacity depends on the desired variety, selection workflow, setup, location, and schedule. Share your team count and deadline so a realistic production plan can be created.

How much space is needed for an on-site setup?

The required footprint depends on the chosen background and lighting. A clear indoor area with reliable power and room between the subject and background is usually best; exact needs are confirmed during planning.

How long does each employee need?

Timing varies with the number of expressions, wardrobe changes, image review, and final-image requirements. The schedule will balance efficiency with a result employees feel good about.

Can you match headshots for future hires?

Yes. Consistent setup notes, background choices, crop, and editing make future matching more practical. Scheduling and location options can be discussed as new employees join.

Can employees choose their favorite image?

A selection workflow can be included. The best approach depends on team size, timeline, and whether your company or each employee approves the final choice.

Do you photograph groups too?

Yes. Leadership, department, and full-team group photographs can be added when the location and schedule support them.

How are team headshots priced?

Quotes account for team size, setup, location, schedule, final images per person, selection method, retouching, file organization, travel, and turnaround.
