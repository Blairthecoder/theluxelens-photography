Frequently Asked Questions

Will you help me pose?

Yes. Ja'Von gives practical direction for posture, shoulders, expression, and small adjustments throughout the session.

What should I wear for a professional headshot?

Choose clothing that fits well and supports the role or audience you have in mind. Wardrobe options and the planned background can be discussed before the session.

Can I use the images for LinkedIn and my company website?

Yes. Share where the photograph will appear so useful crops and delivery formats can be planned.

Do you retouch headshots?

Selected photographs receive a polished finish designed to keep skin texture and personal features looking natural.

How do I book?

Use the live booking page to choose the available headshot offering, or send an inquiry if you need a custom location, deadline, or broader portrait set.

How much does a professional headshot cost in Houston?

A professional headshot is $50 for a 30-minute session at your desired location, with guided posing and 3 final photos, fully edited and retouched.

How long until I receive my headshots?

Headshots are delivered with a 1-day turnaround.

How many final photos do I get?

You receive 3 final headshot photos, fully edited and retouched.

What is the cancellation policy?

24-hour cancellation notice is required for professional headshot sessions.
