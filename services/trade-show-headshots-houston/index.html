Frequently Asked Questions

What events are a good fit for a headshot station?

Professional conferences, association meetings, recruiting events, leadership programs, trade shows, networking events, and sponsor activations can all benefit when attendees value an updated portrait.

How many attendees can be photographed?

Throughput depends on the number of stations, photographers, operating hours, posing variety, selection method, delivery workflow, and space. We will build a realistic estimate from your event details rather than promise an unsupported number.

Can the station include sponsor branding?

Branding can be considered in the background, signage, attendee instructions, or delivery experience when it does not compromise portrait quality. Share sponsor guidelines during planning.

Do attendees receive direction?

Yes. Ja'Von provides concise posing and expression guidance so the experience stays welcoming and efficient.

How are the photographs delivered?

The delivery method depends on event scale, required turnaround, privacy, and organizer needs. Those details are documented before production.

Can you provide more than one station?

Multiple stations or additional photographers may be possible depending on the date, venue, footprint, and production requirements. Request this early.

How is conference headshot coverage priced?

Pricing reflects event hours, planning, stations, photographers, equipment, travel, delivery workflow, image volume, branding, usage, and turnaround. Send the run of show and expected attendance for a custom proposal.
