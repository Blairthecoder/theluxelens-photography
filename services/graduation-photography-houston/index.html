Sessions for High School and College Grads

Graduation sessions work for high school seniors, college graduates, and anyone completing a degree or program worth marking with real portraits instead of a single cap-toss photo from commencement day.

Most graduates want two things from the session: the expected cap-and-gown portrait for family and announcements, and a set of images that reflect their personal style without the gown, useful for social media, a portfolio, or simply a portrait that feels like them.

Planning covers your school colors, whether a second outfit is worth bringing, and whether a campus, a studio, or a Houston location with character fits the feel you want. That decision shapes the whole session more than almost anything else.

Finished images work for announcements, framed gifts for grandparents, social media, and college or scholarship applications that ask for a current portrait.

Planning Your Graduation Session

When to Book

Graduation sessions get busy in the weeks around commencement, so reaching out a month or more ahead gives you the most flexibility with dates and locations.

Cap-and-Gown Images

Bring your gown pressed and your cap free of dents, along with any cords, stoles, or tassels you've earned. A few minutes of steaming beforehand makes a visible difference in how the fabric photographs.

Campus, Studio, and City Locations

A campus session ties the portraits to your specific school. A studio setting gives a clean, timeless backdrop. A Houston city location adds energy if you want the images to feel more editorial than traditional.

Bringing a Second Outfit

Most graduates bring a second look for portraits without the gown, something that reflects their personal style rather than the required uniform of commencement day.

Individual and Group Graduation Sessions

Solo sessions allow more time for different looks and locations. Small-group sessions with classmates or siblings graduating together can be planned with a mix of group and individual portraits.

Serving Houston and the Surrounding Area

Graduation sessions are available across Houston and nearby communities including Sugar Land, Katy, Missouri City, Richmond, Pearland, and Stafford, whether your school is inside the city or in one of the surrounding suburbs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

A month or more ahead is ideal, since graduation sessions cluster around commencement season and dates fill quickly.

Can you help me choose a location?

Yes. Campus, studio, and Houston city locations are all options, and Ja'Von can help you decide based on the look you want.

Do you offer studio and outdoor photography?

Yes. Sessions can be planned indoors for a clean, controlled look or outdoors on campus or around the city.

What should I wear under my cap and gown?

Something simple that won't show much, since the gown covers most of it. Bring a second outfit if you want portraits without the gown.

Can I include multiple outfits?

Yes. Most sessions include time for cap-and-gown portraits plus a second, more personal look.

Do you photograph groups of graduating friends or siblings?

Yes. Small-group sessions can be planned with a mix of group portraits and individual images for each person.

How do I receive my images?

You'll receive a private online gallery with your finished photographs, ready to download and share.

Can the images be used for announcements and applications?

Yes. Finished images work well for graduation announcements, framed gifts, and portraits requested for applications.
