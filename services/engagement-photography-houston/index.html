Frequently Asked Questions

Where can we take engagement photos in Houston?

The right location depends on your style, timing, access, and weather. Ja'Von can help narrow the choice to an elegant interior, a meaningful neighborhood, an outdoor setting, or another approved Houston-area location.

What should we wear?

Choose coordinated looks that feel like you without matching exactly. Wardrobe, footwear, movement, location colors, and the option for more than one look can be discussed while planning.

Can we use the photographs for save-the-dates?

Yes. Mention invitations, save-the-dates, or wedding-website needs before the session so useful orientations and negative space can be included in the shot plan.

Do you photograph surprise proposals?

Yes, when timing, location, access, and a discreet plan can be arranged in advance. Share the idea early so Ja'Von can help plan the photography without giving away the surprise.

Can an engagement session be paired with wedding coverage?

Yes. Ask about engagement and wedding photography together so the coverage and visual direction can be planned as one connected story.
