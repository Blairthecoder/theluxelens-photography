Built for Growing Teams and Organizations

Corporate sessions are built for companies that need more than one type of image: executive portraits for leadership pages, team headshots for a directory, workplace photography for recruiting and culture content, and coverage for conferences or company milestones.

Most organizations booking a corporate session need consistency across a group of people photographed over one session or several, sometimes months apart as new hires join. Planning covers background, lighting setup, and timing so a headshot taken in March matches one taken in October.

The difference shows up in how the day is run. A clear schedule, a consistent setup, and direction that moves each person through quickly keeps a large team session from eating an entire afternoon while still producing images that feel considered rather than rushed.

Finished images are organized and delivered in a way that makes it easy to update a website team page, a recruiting deck, or an internal directory without hunting through hundreds of unsorted files.

Planning a Corporate Session

Executive Portraits

Leadership portraits often carry more visual weight than standard team headshots, worth a slightly longer setup time and a background choice that fits how the company wants to be perceived.

Team Headshots

Consistency is the priority for team headshots: matching background, lighting, and crop across everyone, even if people are photographed on different days as new hires join.

Office and Workplace Photography

Images of your actual workspace and team in action support recruiting pages and culture content better than stock photography, and tend to need less staging than people expect.

Conferences and Corporate Events

Event coverage for a conference or company gathering focuses on speakers, attendees, key moments, and the overall energy of the room, similar in spirit to general event photography but planned around your specific agenda.

Recruiting and Culture Imagery

Photographs that show real people and real workspace, rather than posed stock-style scenes, tend to perform better on careers pages and recruiting materials.

Company Announcements

Product launches, leadership changes, and company milestones often need a dedicated portrait or event photo on short notice, worth planning for in advance if you know the date.

Consistent Visual Standards Across Teams

A documented approach to background, lighting, and framing makes it possible to add new headshots later without the newest hire looking visually out of place next to the rest of the team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

A few weeks ahead is helpful, especially for larger teams or events that need to be scheduled around a company calendar.

Can you photograph a large team or entire department?

Yes. Team sessions are scheduled with a clear plan for consistent background, lighting, and pacing across everyone involved.

Do you offer on-site and studio photography?

Yes. Sessions can be planned at your office, an outside location, or in a studio depending on what the project needs.

How do I inquire about a commercial project?

Send an inquiry describing your team size, timeline, and goals, and Ja'Von will follow up to plan the details.

Can you cover a conference or company event?

Yes. Event coverage can be planned around your specific agenda, speakers, and key moments.

How do you keep headshots consistent if we hire new people later?

A documented approach to background, lighting, and framing is kept on file so new headshots added later match the rest of the team.

Can the images be used for websites, recruiting, and press?

Yes. Images are delivered in formats that work across a website, recruiting materials, internal use, and press requests.

How do I receive our images?

You'll receive a private online gallery, organized for easy use across your organization.
