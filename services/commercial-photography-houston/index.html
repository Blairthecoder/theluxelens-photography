How a Commercial Photography Project Comes Together

1. Planning and Discovery

We identify where the photographs will appear, who needs to respond to them, and which formats your website, campaign, recruiting, press, and social teams require.

2. Shot List and Creative Direction

Your team can provide a brief or collaborate with Ja'Von on a practical shot list covering leadership, employees at work, interactions, details, facilities, products, and brand moments.

3. Photography Session

Production follows the agreed schedule and shot list, with priority images covered first so the plan holds even if time runs short on secondary shots.

4. Image Selection and Editing

You review a curated online gallery and select final images. Retouching and color work are matched to how your brand already presents itself.

5. Delivery and Commercial Use

Project scope depends on locations, subjects, production time, image volume, turnaround, and licensing needs. Final files are delivered in the formats and usage terms set out in your proposal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of companies do you photograph?

The Luxe Lens works with founders, professional-service firms, corporate teams, retailers, product businesses, event organizations, and other Houston companies that need current brand imagery.

Do you photograph employees in real workplaces?

Yes. Workplace photography can show employees doing real work in offices, studios, retail environments, and other approved locations. Safety, access, privacy, and brand requirements are planned before production.

Can you photograph executives and teams during the same project?

Yes. A commercial project can combine executive portraits, employee headshots, workplace storytelling, group photographs, and supporting detail images when the schedule allows.

How is commercial photography priced?

Pricing is based on planning, production time, locations, subjects, image volume, turnaround, assistants or additional production needs, and intended commercial use. Inquire with a brief for a tailored proposal.

Are commercial usage rights included?

The proposal will state the agreed usage clearly. Tell us where, how long, and in which markets the images will be used so the right license can be included.

Can you follow our brand guidelines?

Yes. Share brand standards, reference campaigns, layouts, required crops, and any do-or-don't guidance during planning.

Do you provide fast-turnaround images?

Priority delivery can be discussed for press, launches, and events. Availability and the number of rush images depend on the production schedule.
