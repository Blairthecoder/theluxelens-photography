Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer studio and outdoor sessions?

Yes. Sessions can be planned in a studio for a controlled editorial look or on location for natural light and a stronger sense of place.

Will you help me pose?

Yes. Ja'Von offers clear direction throughout the session, including posture, movement, expression, and small adjustments that photograph well.

Do you photograph families and couples?

Yes. Portrait sessions can be planned for individuals, couples, families, and multigenerational groups, with combinations discussed before the session.

How do we choose a Houston-area location?

Ja'Von can help compare studio and on-location options based on the look, group size, access, weather, and setting you want in the finished photographs.

Can I bring more than one look?

Multiple looks can be planned depending on session length. Include what you have in mind when you inquire so the right amount of time can be reserved.
