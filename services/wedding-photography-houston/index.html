Frequently Asked Questions

When should we inquire?

Houston wedding dates can book well in advance. Reach out as soon as your date and venue are set so availability and coverage can be discussed.

Do you photograph courthouse weddings?

Yes. Ja’Von photographs courthouse weddings, and one courthouse couple described him as “professional, punctual, and had a lot of creative ideas.” Share your ceremony time and location so portraits can be planned around it.

Do you photograph small or intimate weddings?

Coverage is planned around your celebration, so small ceremonies and larger receptions are both welcome. Share your guest count and schedule in your inquiry.

How much does wedding photography cost?

Wedding coverage is customized around your celebration. Send your date, venue, and priorities through the inquiry form and Ja’Von will follow up with details.

What is your wedding photography style?

A blend of documentary observation and calm portrait direction, so the gallery feels complete without making the celebration feel staged.

Do you help with the photography timeline?

Yes. Ja’Von can coordinate photography priorities with your planner or venue so portraits and key moments have enough time without taking over the day. The wedding photography timeline guide is a good starting point.

Will you guide us during portraits?

Yes. Ja’Von provides direction for couple, wedding-party, and family portraits while keeping the day moving and leaving space for natural interaction.

How will we receive our wedding photographs?

Your carefully edited, high-resolution photographs are delivered in a private online gallery for viewing and sharing.

Do you travel outside Houston?

Yes. The Luxe Lens is based in Houston and photographs weddings across Greater Houston, including Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Pearland, Cypress, and Missouri City. Celebrations elsewhere in Texas and destination coverage can be discussed.
