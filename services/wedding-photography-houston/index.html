Frequently Asked Questions

When should we inquire?

Houston wedding dates can book well in advance. Reach out as soon as your date and venue are set so availability and coverage can be discussed.

Do you help with the photography timeline?

Yes. Ja'Von can coordinate photography priorities with your planner or venue so portraits and key moments have enough time without taking over the day.

Will you guide us during portraits?

Yes. Ja'Von provides direction for couple, wedding-party, and family portraits while keeping the day moving and leaving space for natural interaction.

How will we receive our wedding photographs?

Your carefully edited, high-resolution photographs are delivered in a private online gallery for viewing and sharing.

Do you travel outside Houston?

Yes. The Luxe Lens is based in Houston and can discuss celebrations throughout Texas and destination coverage.
