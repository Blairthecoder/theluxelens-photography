Built for Founders, Consultants, and Creatives

Personal branding sessions are built for founders, consultants, coaches, real estate agents, speakers, and creatives who show up as the face of their own business and need more than a single outdated headshot to prove it.

Most clients need a working library rather than one photo: a strong founder portrait, a handful of images that show you actually doing the work, and enough variety in crop and orientation to fill a website, a social feed, and printed materials without repeating the same shot everywhere.

Planning starts with your business goals rather than a generic shot list. What does your website need above the fold? What does your social content calendar actually require week to week? Those answers shape wardrobe, location, and how many setups are worth the time.

Finished images are delivered in both horizontal and vertical orientations where it matters, so the same session can supply a website banner, an Instagram grid, and a printed one-sheet without cropping something awkwardly to fit.

Planning a Personal Branding Session

Planning Images Around Business Goals

Start with what the images need to accomplish, building trust on a website, filling a content calendar, supporting a launch, rather than a generic list of poses. That answer determines almost every other decision.

Website and Social-Media Image Needs

A website usually needs a small number of polished, deliberate shots. Social content benefits from more variety and a mix of posed and working images that can be spread out over weeks without repeating.

Wardrobe and Location Variety

Two or three outfit changes across one or two locations usually produce enough variety without making the day feel rushed. Bring pieces that reflect how you actually dress for work, not just your most formal option.

Founder Portraits

A strong founder portrait is worth dedicating real time to, since it's often the single image that ends up on your homepage, your LinkedIn, and your press mentions all at once.

Working Images

Photographs of you actually doing the work, at a desk, with a client, mid-conversation, tend to perform better in social content than another posed portrait, and they round out the library with images that feel less staged.

Content Libraries and Multiple Formats

Planning for both horizontal and vertical crops during the session, rather than cropping afterward, keeps every image usable across a website banner, a social post, and printed materials without awkward compromises.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

A couple of weeks ahead gives enough time to plan wardrobe, locations, and a shot list around your goals.

Can you help me choose a location?

Yes. Location choice is part of the planning conversation, based on the tone you want and how many setups you have time for.

Do you offer studio and on-location photography?

Yes. Sessions can be planned in a studio, at your workspace, or around Houston depending on what fits your brand.

What should I wear?

Bring a few outfit options that reflect how you actually dress for work. Two or three changes across one or two locations usually works well.

Can I include working or in-action images?

Yes. Photographs of you actually doing the work often round out a personal brand library better than posed portraits alone.

How do I inquire about a personal branding project?

Send an inquiry describing what you do and where the images need to live, and Ja'Von will follow up to plan the details.

Can the images be used for websites, advertising, or social media?

Yes. Images are delivered in horizontal and vertical formats so they work across a website, social content, and printed materials.

How do I receive my images?

You'll receive a private online gallery with your finished, edited image library to view and download.
