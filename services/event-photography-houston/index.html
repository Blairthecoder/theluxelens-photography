Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of events do you photograph?

The Luxe Lens covers private celebrations, performances, launches, community events, galas, and milestone gatherings across Greater Houston.

Can you work from our shot list?

Absolutely. Share priority people, partners, details, and moments before the event so they can be built into the coverage plan.

Do you photograph corporate events?

Yes. Coverage can be planned for company celebrations, launches, networking programs, speakers, performances, and community gatherings in Houston and across the surrounding area.

Can you photograph speakers and group portraits?

Yes. Speaker, performer, sponsor, team, and guided group photographs can be included when they are identified in the coverage plan.

How quickly are event images delivered?

Turnaround depends on event length and image volume. If you need a small set for press or social media quickly, mention that in your inquiry.
