Planning a Birthday Session

Milestone Birthday Sessions

Sweet sixteens, thirtieths, fortieths, and fiftieths are the most common milestone sessions, but any age with meaning to you is worth a dedicated portrait rather than a candid from the party.

Studio Portrait Sessions vs. Event Coverage

A studio session focuses entirely on the birthday person with full control over lighting and background. Event coverage documents the actual party, guests, cake, and candid moments, which is a different kind of gallery altogether.

Props, Wardrobe, and Concepts

A single meaningful prop, balloons in a color that suits the space, a number, a favorite object, tends to read better in photographs than a room full of themed decor. Wardrobe in a color that complements the prop or backdrop keeps the images feeling cohesive.

Planning Enough Time Before the Birthday

Booking a couple of weeks ahead of the actual date gives enough time for the gallery to be ready before invitations, cards, or the party itself need the images.

Choosing a Visual Direction That Doesn't Feel Overly Themed

The strongest milestone portraits use one or two intentional touches rather than a full themed set. A gold balloon and a clean backdrop says "fiftieth birthday" just as clearly as a room full of matching decorations, without dating the photo the way heavy theming can.

Serving Houston and the Surrounding Area

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

A couple of weeks ahead is usually enough, though popular dates around holidays or milestone ages benefit from more lead time.

Do you photograph the birthday party itself, or just portraits?

Both are available. A studio portrait session focuses on the birthday person, while event coverage documents the actual celebration.

What should I wear?

Solid colors that complement your backdrop or props photograph best. Bring a couple of options if you're unsure.

Can I bring props or balloons?

Yes. A prop or two adds a celebratory feel without turning the whole session into a themed shoot.

Can I include family members in the session?

Yes. Family or friends can join for a portion of the session alongside individual portraits of the birthday person.

Do you offer studio and outdoor sessions?

Yes. A studio setting gives the most control over lighting and props, while outdoor sessions add natural light and a Houston backdrop.

How do I receive my images?

You'll receive a private online gallery with your finished, edited photographs to view and download.

Can the images be used for invitations or announcements?

Yes. Let Ja'Von know if you're planning to use an image for an invitation so timing can be planned around it.
