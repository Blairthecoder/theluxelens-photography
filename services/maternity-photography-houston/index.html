Frequently Asked Questions

When should I schedule a maternity session?

Many clients prefer a point when the pregnancy is visibly defined but movement is still comfortable. Your timing and health come first, so inquire early and choose what feels right.

Can my partner or children join?

Yes. Partner and family portraits can be included and planned alongside individual maternity images.

Do you help plan the visual direction?

Yes. Bring references, wardrobe ideas, or simply a feeling you want. Ja'Von will help shape a practical studio or at-home concept.
