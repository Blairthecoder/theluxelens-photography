Sessions for Every Stage of a Relationship

Couples sessions cover engagements, anniversaries, proposals, date-night portraits, and any relationship milestone worth a set of intentional images instead of a stack of phone photos.

Most couples booking a session are a little nervous about being photographed together, especially if it's their first time doing anything like it. Planning covers the occasion, the setting, and how much posing direction feels comfortable versus how much you'd rather just be caught interacting naturally.

The difference shows up in the pacing of the session. Clear posing and movement prompts get you past the stiff, unsure first few minutes, and once you're moving and talking to each other instead of the camera, the more natural moments start showing up on their own.

Finished galleries work for engagement announcements, save-the-dates, anniversary gifts, wall art, and simply having current portraits of the two of you that don't look like they were taken by accident.

Planning a Couples Session

Engagement Sessions

An engagement session usually pairs with a Houston location that means something to you, where you met, got engaged, or simply love spending time, and works well as the source image for save-the-dates.

Anniversaries

An anniversary session is a chance to update portraits that may be years, or a decade, out of date, and to mark a specific number that matters to you both.

Proposals

If a proposal is being planned, Ja'Von can be positioned nearby to document the moment itself, followed by a short portrait session once the surprise is over.

Date-Night Sessions

A date-night session treats the shoot itself as the occasion, dressed up, somewhere you'd actually go, with the portraits as a byproduct of an evening out rather than a separate event.

Guided Posing and Natural Interaction

Clear prompts, walk toward me, whisper something to each other, hold on for one more second, get you both moving instead of freezing up, which is usually where the most genuine expressions come from.

Choosing Meaningful Houston Locations

A location with personal meaning, your neighborhood, where you had a first date, a spot with skyline views, adds a layer the photographs wouldn't have otherwise. Ja'Von can also suggest options if you're not sure where to start.

Serving Houston and the Surrounding Area

Couples sessions are available throughout Houston and nearby communities including Sugar Land, Katy, Missouri City, Richmond, Pearland, and Stafford. Share a location idea when you inquire, or ask for suggestions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

A few weeks ahead is usually enough, though popular Houston locations and weekend dates benefit from more lead time.

Can you help me choose a location?

Yes. Ja'Von can suggest Houston locations based on your style, or work with a spot that already means something to you.

Do you offer studio and outdoor photography?

Yes. Sessions can be planned in a studio for a controlled look or on location around Houston.

What should we wear?

Complementary colors that don't match exactly tend to photograph best. Ja'Von can offer guidance once the location is set.

Is this different from wedding photography?

Yes. Couples sessions cover engagements, anniversaries, and portraits outside of an actual wedding day, which has its own separate coverage.

Can you help with a proposal?

Yes. Ja'Von can be positioned nearby to document a proposal, followed by portraits once the moment has happened.

How do I receive our images?

You'll receive a private online gallery with your finished photographs to view, download, and share.

Can the images be used for save-the-dates or announcements?

Yes. Let Ja'Von know if you're planning to use an image for a save-the-date or announcement so timing can be planned around it.
