Family Sessions for Every Season

Family sessions work for a range of moments: a growing immediate family that hasn't had updated portraits in years, a multigenerational gathering that only happens once or twice a year, a new baby joining older siblings, or a family that simply wants one current image that reflects who they are right now.

Before the session, planning covers who's involved, their ages, and where the finished images need to work, over a fireplace, in a hallway gallery, or as a holiday card. That conversation shapes whether a studio, an outdoor Houston location, or your own home makes the most sense.

The difference shows up in how the session is directed. Rather than lining everyone up and asking for a frozen smile, Ja'Von builds in movement and interaction, a walk, a hug, a shared laugh, so kids stay engaged and the resulting images feel like an actual moment rather than a posed lineup.

Finished galleries are edited for wall art, albums, holiday cards, and everyday sharing, so the same session can produce both a large statement print and the smaller images you send to family.

Planning a Family Session

Coordinating Outfits Without Matching Exactly

A shared color palette reads better in photographs than identical outfits. Pick two or three complementary tones and let everyone wear their own version, rather than putting the whole family in the same shirt.

Preparing Young Children

Sessions for toddlers and young kids go smoother with a plan for snacks, a favorite small toy, and a time of day that avoids nap windows. A little unscripted movement, a piggyback ride, a game of chase, usually produces the most genuine expressions.

Studio, Outdoor, or At-Home Sessions

A studio gives a clean, controlled backdrop that works well for wall art. An outdoor Houston location adds natural light and a sense of place. An at-home session captures your family in the space you actually live in, which some families prefer for a more personal set of images.

Immediate vs. Extended Family Sessions

A smaller immediate family session allows more one-on-one moments and flexibility with poses. A larger extended family gathering needs a bit more structure, groupings planned in advance, so everyone gets photographed together and in smaller combinations without the day running long.

Selecting Meaningful Artwork or Image Uses

Think about where the images will actually live before the session, a large print over a staircase, a gallery wall, a yearly holiday card, so the crop and orientation can be planned rather than figured out after the fact.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book a family session?

Booking a few weeks ahead gives the most flexibility with dates, especially around holidays when family schedules fill up quickly.

Can you help me choose a location?

Yes. Studio, outdoor, and at-home options are all available, and Ja'Von can suggest a Houston location based on your family's style.

Do you offer studio and outdoor photography?

Yes. Sessions can be planned in a controlled studio setting or on location for natural light and a stronger sense of place.

What should everyone wear?

A shared color palette works better than matching outfits. Bring a couple of color options and Ja'Von can help you settle on the final choice.

Can I include grandparents or extended family?

Yes. Extended family sessions are planned with groupings worked out in advance so everyone gets photographed together and in smaller combinations.

How do I prepare young kids for the session?

Plan around nap schedules, bring a small snack or toy, and expect some of the best images to come from unscripted moments rather than posed ones.

How do I receive my images?

You'll receive a private online gallery with your finished, edited photographs to view and download.

Can the images be used for prints and wall art?

Yes. Let Ja'Von know if you're planning a large print or gallery wall so the session can be shot with that crop in mind.
