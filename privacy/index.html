Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

The Luxe Lens collects the information you choose to submit through the photography inquiry form, such as your name, email address, phone number, preferred date, location, and project details.

How Information Is Used

Inquiry information is used to respond to your request, discuss photography services, and coordinate potential bookings. The inquiry form does not add you to a marketing newsletter.

Form Processing

This site is hosted on Netlify. Netlify processes form submissions on behalf of The Luxe Lens. Information may also be handled by service providers used to communicate with you or deliver photography services.

Website Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, referral sources, approximate location, browser and device information, and interactions with the website. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to provide this service. This information is used to evaluate website performance and improve the visitor experience. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

External Links

The website links to Pixieset, Airbnb, Facebook, Instagram, and Google. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted by emailing javon.williams@theluxephotography.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to javon.williams@theluxephotography.com or by calling (713) 565-9033.
