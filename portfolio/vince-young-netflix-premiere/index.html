Event photography case study · Houston

Netflix Untold: The Testimony of Vince Young

Eight hours of premiere coverage at River Oaks Theatre, moving from a custom burnt-orange carpet to backstage access and a live on-stage discussion.

The assignment

One Event. Three Distinct Environments.

The Luxe Lens documented the private screening premiere from arrivals through the theater program, building a cohesive visual record across changing light, pace, and access.

Coverage included Vince Young, Adrian Peterson, Andre Johnson, Bun B, Major Johnson Finley, Loreal Sarkisian, Blake Fertitta, and other invited guests.

Coverage
Eight continuous hours
Venue
River Oaks Theatre · Houston
Deliverables
Carpet portraits, backstage moments, and live program coverage

The photographic approach

Built for Pace, Access, and Story

Each part of the evening required a different visual and technical response while the finished collection still needed to feel like one event.

01 · Arrivals

Fast-Paced Portraiture

Consistent, polished step-and-repeat portraits on the custom burnt-orange carpet during a high-volume guest arrival window.

02 · Backstage

Exclusive Access

Natural green-room and behind-the-scenes photographs that preserve the relationships and atmosphere beyond the public-facing premiere.

03 · On Stage

Low-Light Storytelling

Clean theater photography of the live panel, speaker moments, and audience-facing program under challenging stage light.
