How to Choose a Houston Photographer: 8 Things to Check Before You Book

The right photographer is more than a portfolio. Compare session length, edited photo counts, turnaround, direction, and cancellation terms so the price you see matches the experience you get.

  1. Home
  2. Journal
  3. How to Choose a Houston Photographer

Written by Ja'Von Williams, Houston photographer

Published September 29, 2026 · 6 minute read

1. Look for Complete Stories, Not Just Highlights

A strong portfolio shows the same standard across different sessions, lighting, and people, not one lucky shoot. Look for full sessions that hold together from the first frame to the last, and check that the work resembles what you want, whether that is studio portraits, lifestyle sessions, events, or weddings. Start with the portfolio by session.

2. Read Reviews for What Actually Happened

Star ratings matter less than the detail inside the reviews. Look for mentions of punctuality, direction, patience, and how the session felt. Clients of The Luxe Lens describe Ja’Von as “professional, punctual,” “patient,” and “intentional with every minute,” and returning clients have booked a second session and a group shoot with friends. You can read the reviews on Google.

3. Ask Exactly What Is Included

Two sessions at similar prices can be very different. Ask for session length, number of outfits, number of fully edited photos, and whether travel is included. Here is what each Luxe Lens session includes:

  • Studio, $175: 1 hour in the studio, 1–2 outfits, 15 fully edited photos, 3-day turnaround.
  • Lifestyle, $100: 1 hour at your chosen location, 1–2 outfits, 15 fully edited photos, 3-day turnaround.
  • Events, $300: 2-hour minimum, 30 fully edited photos, 3-day turnaround, travel included.
  • Professional headshots, $50: 30 minutes at your location, guided posing throughout, 3 final photos fully edited and retouched, 1-day turnaround.

Compare the price per fully edited photo, not just the session fee, and check that travel, retouching, and turnaround are not extra charges.

4. Get the Turnaround in Writing

“Soon” is not a delivery date. If photos are needed for a launch, a profile update, or a post the same week, confirm the number of days before you book. Headshots from The Luxe Lens come back in 1 day, and studio, lifestyle, and event galleries in 3 days.

5. Check for Guided Posing and Direction

Most people are not used to being photographed, and a good photographer solves that. Ask how the session is directed. Reviewers of The Luxe Lens highlight the “creative ideas,” help with poses, and a relaxed atmosphere, and professional headshot sessions include guided posing from start to finish.

6. Understand the Cancellation Terms

Life changes plans, so know the policy before you pay. The Luxe Lens asks for 48 hours notice on studio, lifestyle, and event sessions and 24 hours on professional headshots.

7. Match the Session to the Purpose

A headshot for a company bio needs different planning than a family session or a launch party. Choose a photographer who asks how the images will be used and builds the plan around that. See the options for professional headshots, portrait and studio sessions, and event photography.

8. Confirm Booking Is Simple

You should be able to see availability, book, and reach the photographer directly. The Luxe Lens books through an online booking page, and questions before booking are welcome through the contact form.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Houston photography session cost?

Pricing depends on the session type and length. The Luxe Lens sessions start at $50 for a 30-minute professional headshot, $100 for a one-hour lifestyle session, $175 for a one-hour studio session, and $300 for two hours of event coverage.

How long does it take to get photos back from a Houston photographer?

It varies by photographer and session type. At The Luxe Lens, studio, lifestyle, and event photos are delivered within 3 days, and professional headshots within 1 day.

How many edited photos should I expect from a portrait session?

Ask for an exact number, not a range. A one-hour studio or lifestyle session with The Luxe Lens includes 15 fully edited photos, and event coverage includes 30.

Is travel included for event photography in Houston?

Confirm this before booking. Travel is included with The Luxe Lens event coverage, which has a 2-hour minimum.

What is the cancellation policy for a photo session?

Cancellation terms differ by photographer. The Luxe Lens requires 48 hours notice for studio, lifestyle, and event bookings and 24 hours for professional headshots.
