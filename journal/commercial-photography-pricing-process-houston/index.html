How Much Does Commercial Photography Cost in Houston?

Commercial photography pricing in Houston depends on scope, not guesswork. Here is what drives the cost, what is included, and how the process works from consult to delivery.

  1. Home
  2. Journal
  3. How Much Does Commercial Photography Cost in Houston?

Written by Ja'Von Williams, Houston photographer

Published September 28, 2026 · 6 minute read

Quick Overview

Commercial photography in Houston typically runs from a few hundred dollars for a short single-location shoot to several thousand for a full-day, multi-location production. Pricing depends on shoot length, number of locations, image count, and how the photos will be used. Most clients work with The Luxe Lens through a short consult, a planned shot list, a shoot day, and a delivered gallery within about a week.

What Counts as Commercial Photography

Commercial photography covers the images a business uses to market itself: product shots, workplace and facility imagery, brand lifestyle photos, and advertising visuals. It is a different service from corporate headshots, which focus on individual portraits of team members, and from event photography, which documents a specific occasion.

Many Houston businesses need a mix of these. If you are unsure which service fits, read our corporate headshots vs. business photography comparison before booking.

Pricing by Shoot Scope

Three scopes cover most Houston commercial projects:

  • Single-location, half-day: Best for a small business needing a core set of brand and product images in one location. Lower cost, faster turnaround.
  • Full-day, multi-location: Fits businesses with several offices, storefronts, or a mix of product and lifestyle shots across sites.
  • Ongoing brand retainer: For companies that need fresh content on a monthly or quarterly cadence to keep marketing and social channels current.

Exact rates depend on scope, so the fastest way to get a number is a short call walking through what the images are for.

What’s Included

A commercial photography package typically includes a set number of fully edited, high-resolution images, a private online gallery for review and download, and usage rights scoped to how the photos will be used. On-site shoots at your business location are common, though studio sessions are available for product-focused work.

The Process

Most Houston commercial projects move through four stages: a short consult to define the goal, a planned shot list so shoot day runs efficiently, the shoot itself with room for a few unplanned moments, and delivery through a private gallery, usually within about a week. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a commercial photoshoot cost in Houston?

Pricing depends on shoot length, number of locations, and how many final images you need. A short consult is the fastest way to get an accurate quote for your specific project.

Do I own the usage rights to commercial photos?

Usage rights are scoped to your project and confirmed before the shoot, covering where and how the images can be used, such as your website, ads, or social channels.

How long does a commercial photography session take?

Most single-location sessions run a half day. Larger projects covering multiple locations or a longer shot list can run a full day.

What's the difference between commercial and corporate photography?

Commercial photography covers marketing and brand imagery like products, spaces, and lifestyle shots. Corporate photography usually refers to individual headshots of your team.
