Corporate Headshots or Business Photography? How to Choose

Two different services solve two different problems. Here is how to tell which one your Houston team needs, and when to book both.

  1. Home
  2. Journal

Written by Ja'Von Williams, Houston photographer

Published October 1, 2026 · 5 minute read

Quick Overview

Corporate headshots are individual portraits of your team, used on LinkedIn, your website’s about page, and press materials. Business photography is broader, covering your office, product, and brand imagery for marketing and advertising. Many Houston companies need both, and booking them in the same session is usually more cost-effective than scheduling separately.

Corporate Headshots: What They’re For

A headshot answers one question: who is this person? Houston companies use them for LinkedIn profiles, staff directory pages, press bios, and speaker materials. A good headshot session is efficient, especially with a full team, and produces a consistent look across everyone photographed.

Business Photography: What It’s For

Business photography, sometimes called commercial photography, covers everything beyond the individual portrait: your workspace, your product, your team working, and lifestyle imagery that shows what your company actually does. This is the content that fills a website homepage, a sales deck, or a social feed. See our commercial photography pricing guide for cost detail.

Side-by-Side Comparison

Focus: Corporate headshots capture individual team portraits. Business photography captures your workspace, product, and brand imagery.

Typical use: Corporate headshots go on LinkedIn, website bios, and press materials. Business photography fills your website, ads, social, and sales materials.

Session length: Corporate headshots are efficient and scale with team size. Business photography varies with the shot list and number of locations.

Best paired with: Corporate headshots suit team announcements and new hires. Business photography suits rebrands, product launches, and marketing pushes.

Combining Both in One Shoot Day

Because a photographer is already on-site for headshots, most Houston companies save time and money by capturing office and brand imagery in the same session. This is worth flagging during your consult, since it changes the shot list and schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between corporate headshots and business photography?

Corporate headshots are individual portraits of your team members. Business photography covers broader marketing imagery like your workspace, products, and brand content.

How often should a company update team headshots?

Most companies refresh headshots every one to two years, or sooner after major hires, a rebrand, or a change in company presentation.

Can I book both headshots and marketing photos in one session?

Yes. Many Houston clients combine both in a single shoot day, which is more efficient than scheduling two separate sessions.
