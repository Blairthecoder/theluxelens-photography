Before you inquire

Pricing Questions

Why are exact prices not listed?

Coverage depends on the session type, date, location, timeline, number of people, production needs, turnaround, and commercial usage. A tailored proposal keeps the scope and total clear before booking.

What should I include in my inquiry?

Share the photography type, preferred date, location or city, approximate number of people, how the images will be used, and any deadline. Inspiration and a rough shot list are welcome.

Can I book immediately?

Available standard sessions can be reserved through Pixieset. Weddings, events, teams, and commercial projects should begin with an inquiry so the right coverage can be confirmed.

Do you work outside Houston?

Greater Houston is the primary service area. Travel can be discussed for the right project and will be shown clearly in the proposal.
